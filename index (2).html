NTCC · UG Short Internship

Impact of Social Media Exposure on Academic Performance

Amity School of Applied Sciences, Lucknow · Supervisor: Dr. Shailja Pandey

A quantitative survey-based study examining how social media consumption patterns correlate with academic outcomes across 158 respondents from diverse age groups and educational levels. Hypothesis testing via Chi-Square Goodness of Fit and Test of Independence provided statistically rigorous conclusions. Key finding: YouTube is the most educationally impactful platform, and social media enhances faculty-student communication without significantly improving academic performance.

Statistical Analysis of Infant Mortality Rate in India & Uttar Pradesh

Published in: Advanced Statistical and Computational Techniques in Interdisciplinary Research
Co-author: Gunjan Singh (Asst. Professor) · Amity School of Applied Sciences, Lucknow

A published research chapter providing a comprehensive multi-level statistical analysis of India's Infant Mortality Rate (IMR) from 2007–2023, with a focused investigation into Uttar Pradesh and its 19 sub-centers (2011–12). Combines Pareto Analysis, regression modelling, scatter plots, box plots, and standard deviation analysis to identify causes, regional disparities, and future projections — equipping policymakers with evidence-backed intervention strategies.

NTCC · UG Project

Predicting Box Office Success of Movies Using Machine Learning

Amity School of Applied Sciences, Lucknow · Supervisor: Dr. Gunjan Singh · Head: Dr. Asita Kulshreshtha

A rigorous machine learning study using the IMDb Top 1000 Movies dataset to forecast box office revenue. Linear Regression and Random Forest Regressor were applied to features including runtime, user rating, vote count, and Metascore. Factor analysis identified audience preferences and directorial impact as dominant contributors to box office performance. The study benchmarks model accuracy via MSE and R², exposing the non-linear complexity of revenue forecasting in the entertainment industry.
